Undergraduate Degree • CICS BSCS

Bachelor of Science in Computer Science

Department of Computer Studies • De La Salle University – Dasmariñas

4 Years Full-Time PAASCU Level IV Accredited 2 Specialization Tracks Industry Practicum

Program Description

The BSCS program prepares students to be highly competent and certified in the areas of computing theory and applications.

It trains them in abstract reasoning, analytical thinking, and research, with specialized tracks in Robotics, Mobile, and Game Development.

Available Specialization Tracks:
Game Development Intelligent Systems

Career Opportunities

Graduates of the BSCS program pursue roles such as:

Application / Game Developer CS Instructor Researcher Trainer Database Programmer / Designer Mobile Application Developer Systems Analyst
View Program Goals & Comprehensive Career Pathways ▼
Program Goals
Apply concepts of computing and mathematics in different domains of computer science.
Design, develop, test and evaluate various solutions using algorithms for complex computing problems that take full advantage of the usability of computer resources.
Document computer-based systems by writing effective reports for risk-assessment impact.
Communicate effectively with computing and non-computer science professionals about computing activities.
Recognize the legal, social, ethical, and professional issues involved in the utilization of computer technology and address these issues.
Engage in continuing professional development through performing independent or collaborative research as computing professionals.

Undergraduate Degree • CICS BSIT

Bachelor of Science in Information Technology

Department of Information Technology • De La Salle University – Dasmariñas

4 Years Full-Time PAASCU Level IV Accredited 2 Specialization Tracks 486-Hour Practicum

Program Description

The BSIT program focuses on the rigorous training of students with the latest IT concepts and industry applications.

It provides a practical, industry-driven approach to studying the various facets and latest technologies of the IT landscape, supported by cutting-edge laboratory facilities.

Available Specialization Tracks:
Network Technology Software Technology

Career Opportunities

Graduates of the BSIT program excel in high-demand roles:

Database Administrator IT Entrepreneur Information Security Administrator IT Instructor Programmer Trainer / Researcher Network Administrator Technical Support Specialist Web Administrator Webmaster Web Developer Network Engineer
View Program Goals & Comprehensive Career Pathways ▼
Program Goals
Apply concepts of computing in different domains of information technology.
Identify and analyze the problem and define the computing requirements appropriate to its solution.
Design, develop, test and evaluate various solutions using modern computing tools for complex computing problems.
Document the computer-based systems by writing effective reports for end-users.
Work effectively as an individual or as a member of a team during software development for company-based systems.
Manage and maintain system security, reliability, and efficiency.
Communicate effectively with the computing community and non-IT professionals about computing activities.
Recognize the legal, social, ethical, and professional issues involved in the utilization of computer technology and address these issues.
Engage in continuing professional development through performing independent or collaborative research as computing professionals.
